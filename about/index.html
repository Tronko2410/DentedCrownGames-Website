About

Big ambitions. A few dents.

Dented Crown Games is an independent game development studio based in Austria.

We’re building games around distinctive worlds, memorable characters, and mechanics that make every decision matter.

Our debut project, All in Debt!, combines casino management, cooperative chaos, and a darkly humorous story about owing the wrong people a million euros.

Dented Crown is currently a one-person studio. We believe personality, meaningful gameplay, and attention to detail matter more than piling on features. We’re just getting started.

How we work

Our principles

Originality

Our own visual identity, our own characters, and ideas you haven’t played a hundred times.

Gameplay first

Great visuals help. But if it isn’t fun to play, it isn’t done.

Personality

Humor, recognizable characters, and little surprises hidden in the details.

Meaningful choices

Decisions should have consequences — no busywork, no filler.

Quality over deadlines

We won’t ship something unfinished just to hit a date.

Community

We take feedback from real players seriously — it makes the games better.
