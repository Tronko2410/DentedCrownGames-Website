Why “Dented Crown”?

Not every crown stays perfect.

A crown stands for ambition — aiming high and going for it. The dent stands for everything that happens on the way there: mistakes, setbacks, chaos.

That’s where the best stories come from, and that’s the kind of game we want to make.
