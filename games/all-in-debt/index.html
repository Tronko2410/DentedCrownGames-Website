The setup

You borrowed big. You lost bigger.

It started with a bad idea on a sad couch: borrow some money, win it all back at the casino. Two Mafia brothers were happy to help. One million euros later, the money is gone — and they’re waiting at your door.

A black bag, a car ride, and one very clear explanation later, you’re standing in front of a rundown casino. It’s yours to run now. Every cent it makes goes toward what you owe.

FAQ

Questions?

What is All in Debt!?

An upcoming casino management game where you build and run a casino while trying to repay a million euros to the Mafia.

Can I play alone?

Yes. A complete single-player experience is planned, including offline play.

Does it support co-op?

Online co-op for up to four players is planned. You’ll manage the same casino, share your finances, and face the same debt.

Can I customize my character?

Yes — character customization is a planned feature, so you can create your own stylized character and use it across games.

Where will it be available?

All in Debt! is being developed for Windows PC, with Steam planned as the main release platform.

When is it coming out?

The game is in early development. We’ll announce a release date once we’re confident in the schedule.

Is there a demo?

Not yet. Playtests and a possible public demo are planned for a later stage of development.

Is there real-money gambling or real crypto?

No. Casino games and the in-game currency DirtCoin are entirely fictional. Nothing can be paid out or exchanged for real money.

All in Debt! is a work in progress. Features, content, and visuals shown or described here are subject to change.
